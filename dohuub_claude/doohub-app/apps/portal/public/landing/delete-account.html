Delete Your Account

Request permanent deletion of your DoHuub account and data.

What happens when you delete your account?

When you request account deletion, the following will be permanently removed within 30 days:

  • Your profile information (name, email, phone number, photo)
  • Your saved addresses
  • Your booking history
  • Your reviews and ratings
  • Your rewards points, streaks, and milestones
  • Your referral history
  • Your saved payment methods

This action is irreversible. Once your account is deleted, it cannot be recovered.

Before you delete

  • Complete or cancel any pending bookings first
  • Redeem any unused rewards points, as they will be forfeited
  • Download any data you wish to keep by contacting privacy@dohuub.com

Request Account Deletion

You can delete your account using any of these methods:

Method 1: In the App

Open DoHuub > Profile > Settings > Delete Account

Method 2: Email Request

Send an email to privacy@dohuub.com from the email address associated with your account with the subject line "Account Deletion Request."

Method 3: Web Form

Questions?

If you have any questions about account deletion or data handling, please contact us at privacy@dohuub.com or visit our Help Center.
