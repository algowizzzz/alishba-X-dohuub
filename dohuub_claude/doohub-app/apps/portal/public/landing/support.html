Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create an account?

Download DoHuub from the App Store or Google Play, open the app, and tap "Get Started." You can sign up with your email address and create a password. Follow the on-screen steps to complete your profile setup.

How do I book a service?

From the home screen, select a service category (e.g., Cleaning, Handyman, Beauty). Browse available providers, select one, choose your preferred date and time, and confirm your booking. You'll receive a confirmation notification.

How do I cancel a booking?

Go to "My Bookings" in the app, find the booking you want to cancel, and tap "Cancel Booking." Please note that cancellation policies vary by service provider and may include cancellation fees if cancelled close to the scheduled time.

What payment methods are accepted?

DoHuub accepts major credit/debit cards and digital payment methods. All payments are processed securely through encrypted payment processors. You can manage your payment methods in Profile > Payment Methods.

How does the rewards program work?

You earn points with every completed booking. Build daily streaks by booking regularly, reach milestones for bonus rewards, and refer friends to earn additional points. Points can be redeemed for discounts on future bookings.

How do I leave a review?

After a service is completed, you'll receive a prompt to rate and review your experience. You can also go to "My Bookings," find the completed booking, and tap "Leave Review."

How do I delete my account?

You can request account deletion through the app (Profile > Settings > Delete Account) or through our web account deletion page. Your data will be permanently deleted within 30 days of the request.

Is my personal information safe?

Yes. We use industry-standard encryption and security measures to protect your data. Please review our Privacy Policy for complete details on how we handle your information.

How do I report a problem with a service?

If you experience any issues with a service, please contact us at support@dohuub.com with your booking details. We take all reports seriously and will work to resolve the issue promptly.
